Last updated: September 2026

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide our website design and hosting services.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate — for example, our payment processor (Stripe) and website hosting — and only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Your card details are handled directly by Stripe under their security standards and privacy policy. We receive confirmation of payment but not your full card number.

Data retention & security

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect it, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.
